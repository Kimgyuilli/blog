# Reflection and perspective

**Reader question:** What did this experience change, or what position is the author arguing?

For a retrospective, identify the original goal, the consequential events or decisions, the outcome, and what the author would repeat or change. Anchor lessons in a specific event rather than broad advice.

For a perspective piece, state the claim, the experience or evidence behind it, a meaningful counterpoint, and the conditions where the claim applies. Keep the author's actual uncertainty. Do not create a stronger opinion than the source expresses or invent autobiographical details to make the narrative complete.
